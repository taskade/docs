## What's New:

### CSV Export for Mind Map, Board, Calendar & Org Chart

The project **Export** menu now offers CSV in Mind Map, Board, Calendar, and Org Chart views — matching the CSV export List view already had. The download is a flat CSV of the task tree (`depth`, `id`, `text`, `note`, `completed`), with hierarchy preserved through the depth column; custom-field columns are not part of this flat format. Like Markdown export, CSV is available on all plans. See [Project Views](../../../genesis-living-system-builder/workspaces/project-views.md).

***

### Checkbox Field for Flow Forms

Form-trigger automation flows gain a **Checkbox** (boolean) field type. Add it in the form builder alongside text, number, and date inputs; every submission carries an explicit `true`/`false`, so downstream filter and branch steps can act on it without empty-value ambiguity. See [AI Forms](../../../genesis-living-system-builder/automation/ai-forms.md).

***

### Self-Serve Custom Domain Reassignment

You can now move a custom domain from one Space App to another yourself — no support ticket needed. Open **Edit Space → Domains**, pick the domain, and choose a destination workspace; the picker lists workspaces where you have permission to manage custom domains. Works for both Taskade-managed domains and domains you brought yourself. See [Put Your App on Your Own Domain](../../../genesis-living-system-builder/space-apps-guide/custom-domains.md).
