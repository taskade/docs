## What's New:

### Generated Apps Get Real Persistent Auth

Sign-in in Genesis-built apps is now guaranteed to be real: if a build or edit ever produces a login screen that doesn't actually authenticate, EVE detects it and replaces it with working, persistent authentication — and tells you it did. Standard logins default to [GenesisAuth](../../../genesis-living-system-builder/community-and-sharing/genesis-auth.md) (sign in with your Taskade account); apps that manage their own user directory get secure account storage with hashed credentials. See also [Add Login & Roles](../../../genesis-living-system-builder/run-your-business/add-login-and-roles.md).
