## What's New:

### Document OCR Fallback for Scanned & Image-Only Files

**Convert File to Text** and Genesis document extraction now fall back to AI vision OCR when a document has no embedded text layer — scanned PDFs, image-only PDFs, and photo formats like HEIC now extract text instead of failing. The fallback only engages when standard text extraction finds nothing, bills a flat AI-credit fee per document, and respects your plan's AI usage limits; documents with a normal text layer are processed exactly as before. See [Automation Actions](../../../genesis-living-system-builder/automation/actions.md).

***

### Fix: EVE-Built Flows Fill Every Field They Promise

Automation flows authored by Taskade EVE that use AI structured-output steps (reports, analyses, custom-field fills) now reliably fill every field they promise — previously, some generated flows could run successfully yet leave their target fields empty. See the [Workflow Generator](../../../genesis-living-system-builder/automation/workflow-generator.md).
