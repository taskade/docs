# Quick Start Guide

## What is Taskade?

Taskade is an AI workspace. Your projects, AI agents, and automations work together in one place. Taskade Genesis builds a live app from one plain-English prompt. You do not write code.

**Start here:**

| I want to… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Build my first app | [Build Your First App](#build-your-first-app-with-taskade-genesis-5-minutes) on this page |
| Copy a ready-made app | [Taskade App Factory](https://www.taskade.com/apps) |
| Manage tasks and projects | [Just want to manage projects?](#just-want-to-manage-projects-no-app-building-needed) |

***

## How Taskade Is Organized

| Level | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | Your top-level home. It has its own billing and members. | "My Company" |
| **Apps** (Folders) | A group of projects. Each one can become a published app. | "Customer Portal" |
| **Projects** | Your data: tasks, notes, documents, and databases. | "Lead Pipeline" |

Each project holds **tasks**. A task is one line. It can have subtasks, a due date, an assignee, and notes.

Three parts work together in every workspace:

* **Memory**: your projects and data
* **Intelligence**: AI agents that read your data and answer from it
* **Execution**: automations that do repeat work for you, at all hours

Taskade calls these three parts Workspace DNA. Read more in [Workspace DNA](https://www.taskade.com/learn/genesis/workspace-dna).

## Create Your Account (1 minute)

1. [**Sign up for free →**](https://taskade.com/signup)
2. If a teammate invited you, sign up from the link in the invite email.
3. Wait for your workspace home to open. Taskade makes the workspace for you.

Your workspace home shows a prompt box. You describe what you want to build in that box.

A Free workspace has 2 members, 3 Taskade Genesis apps, and 1 agent. A new Free account starts with 0 AI credits. On Free, you earn free AI credits with referrals and invites. You can upgrade at any time.

## Build Your First App with Taskade Genesis (5 minutes)

You describe an app in plain English. Taskade Genesis builds the screens, the data, and the automations. You can also [start your first app with Taskade Genesis](https://www.taskade.com/create) from your workspace.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Choose Your App Idea**

Pick a tool that you need this week:

| App Idea | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer Feedback App** | Collects reviews and alerts a manager |
| **Booking System** | Lets customers book appointments online |
| **Inventory Tracker** | Tracks stock and sends reorder alerts |
| **Lead Manager** | Collects sales leads and sorts them |
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Find the Prompt Box**

Your workspace home is where you start Taskade Genesis. You do not open a separate tab. The prompt box is at the center of your workspace home. Your projects and folders show as **app tiles** below it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Describe Your App**

Fill in this template. Give specific details:

```
I run a [BUSINESS TYPE] and need [USERS] to [ACTION].
They should be able to [SPECIFIC FEATURES].
When [TRIGGER EVENT], automatically [AUTOMATED ACTION].
```

{% hint style="success" %}
**Example prompt:** "I run a restaurant and need customers to submit feedback about their dining experience. They should be able to rate food quality, service speed, and overall satisfaction on a 1-5 scale, plus leave optional comments and upload photos. When someone gives us 3 stars or lower, automatically send a Slack message to our manager channel so we can follow up personally."
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Watch Taskade Genesis Build Your App**

1. Type or paste your prompt in the prompt box.
2. Click the **Send** arrow, or press Enter.
3. Wait 1 to 2 minutes. Your new app shows as a tile below the prompt box.

Taskade Genesis makes the data structure, the screens, the automations, and the AI agents. If your Free account never had AI credits, Taskade EVE shows **Pick a plan to start building** at your first prompt.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Test Your App**

1. Click your new app tile to open the app.
2. Look at the preview beside the chat. It shows your app.
3. Use the app as a real user does. Submit data and open each screen.
4. Open the app on a phone-size screen.
5. Make sure that your connected services (Slack, email) get the alerts.
6. To change the app, chat with Taskade EVE in the left panel.

On a new app, Taskade saves each change as a draft. Visitors see a change only after you click **Publish** again.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**Well done.** You built a working business app.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**No plan yet?** Clone a free app kit from the [Taskade App Factory](https://www.taskade.com/apps). A free kit clones at no cost, and the copy is yours to change.
{% endhint %}

## Just want to manage projects? (No app building needed)

You do not have to build apps. Taskade also works as a project tool:

1. **Create a project**: Open a workspace or a folder, then click **New Project**. Type a title, then start to type in the project.
2. **Add tasks**: Each line is a task. Press TAB to indent a subtask.
3. **Switch views**: Use **List**, **Board**, **Table**, and **Mind Map** at the top of a project. **Docs**, **Calendar**, **Gantt Chart**, **Actionsheet**, and **Org Chart** are under **More views**. Gantt Chart, Actionsheet, and Org Chart need a paid plan. The timeline is part of Gantt Chart.
4. **Assign and schedule**: Click the **+** icon next to a task to open Add-Ons. Set a due date, assign the task, add a comment, or upload a file.
5. **Work as a team**: Invite teammates by email. Each person gets a role, from Owner to Viewer.
6. **Use AI**: Type `/` in a project to open the AI commands. You can also make a custom [AI Agent](../genesis-living-system-builder/ai-features/ai-agents-getting-started.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
**Do you have data in another tool?** You can import projects from Notion, Trello, Asana, and other tools. Read [Import & Export](../help-center/import-and-export.md).
{% endhint %}
